## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-01

First public release.

### Added

- **Accounts and bank sync.** Checking, savings, card, investment, cash, loan and other accounts. European bank connections through Enable Banking with consent renewal and expiry warnings, a daily automatic sync that respects PSD2 call limits, and a manual "Sync now". Balance snapshots and drift detection.
- **Trading 212.** Read-only connection with an API key: cash, holdings, dividends and history.
- **Statement import.** CSV, XLSX and OFX with saved column formats, deduplication and manual accounts and transactions.
- **Transactions.** Search and filters, categories and groups seeded from templates in five languages, rules, merchant memory, a review inbox, automatic matching of transfers between your own accounts, pending-to-booked replacement, and recurring payment detection.
- **AI categorisation (optional).** An Anthropic model categorises what rules and memory left over, with confidence, review and undo, plus merchant names and logos (fetched through Google's favicon service, which can be switched off). IBANs, card numbers, emails, phone numbers and long digit runs are removed before anything is sent.
- **Budgets and saving lines.** An annual budget with income, expense and saving lines linked to categories and accounts, budget versus actual by line, group and month, saving lines funded by account or pie, a transfer plan from the main account, a spreadsheet import, and budget alerts.
- **Goals.** Savings goals with progress and projections.
- **Net worth.** Assets (property, vehicles, investments, valuables) and liabilities (mortgages, loans, debts) with value history, mortgage schedules, depreciation, sale and write-off, credit cards, and monthly net-worth snapshots. Optional monthly AI valuation of assets from comparable listings on configurable portals.
- **Insights.** Spending charts, trends, cash flow, anomaly detection and an optional AI-written monthly report.
- **MCP server for Claude.** Connect claude.ai (OAuth) or Claude Code and other clients (personal access tokens) to read, and optionally write, with read-only or read-and-write scope, audit trail and one-click revoke.
- **PWA and push notifications.** Installable app, offline read-only shell and Web Push alerts for budgets, failed syncs and expiring consents.
- **Households.** Several members per household with owner, member and viewer roles, invitations by link or email, two-step verification (required for owners) with recovery codes, sign-in throttling and an audit log.
- **Languages and branding.** Five interface languages (English, Portuguese (pt-PT), Spanish (es-ES), French (fr-FR) and Italian (it-IT)), light and dark themes, and white-label name, colour and logo by environment variable.
- **Privacy mode.** A toggle in the app shell masks every monetary amount on screen, and the choice is remembered.
- **Per-connection account selection.** Choose which accounts a running bank connection syncs, and change it later without reconnecting.
- **Services settings page.** Owners configure the Anthropic key and models, the Enable Banking application, valuation portals and email in **Settings > Services**, without editing files. Values are stored encrypted, never shown again, and an environment variable always takes precedence.
- **Self-hosting.** A single container image (`web`, `worker`, `migrate`) with a Docker Compose stack and a one-command secrets script, and a Helm chart with optional bundled PostgreSQL, backups to S3-compatible storage, Ingress and NetworkPolicy. Containers run as non-root on a read-only filesystem.
- **Documentation.** Guides for installing, configuring, bank sync, AI, MCP, backup and upgrade, and the architecture.
